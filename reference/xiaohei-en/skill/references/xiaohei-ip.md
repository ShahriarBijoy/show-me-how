# The Xiaohei IP

## Character definition

Xiaohei ("Little Black") is the fixed visual IP for Ian's body illustrations.

By default every image includes Xiaohei. Xiaohei is not a mascot, not a sticker, not a cute decoration, but an absurd worker seriously participating in the running of the system.

## Appearance

- Small solid-black monster.
- White round dot eyes.
- Thin legs, occasionally thin arms.
- Body can be a cylinder, black bean, black box, funnel, shadow, hole, or a black block inside a machine.
- Slightly irregular outline, hand-drawn feel.
- Expression: blank, dazed, calm, serious.

## Personality

- Very serious, but doing something slightly absurd.
- Like a low-key system operator.
- Deadpan humor, never plays cute.
- A bit clumsy, but not stupid.
- Looks like it is genuinely responsible for some job in a whiteboard sketch.

## Common duties

Let Xiaohei carry the core action:

- Carrying materials.
- Pulling lines to converge information sources.
- Stuck in a breakpoint.
- Operating the "judgment" lever inside a machine.
- Becoming a filtering funnel.
- Cutting open the "material fish".
- Stamping handoff scripts.
- Leading the handoff path.
- Holding a warning sign while looking at pitfalls.
- Reaching out of a hole but failing to catch the content.
- Nearby: hauling bricks, building bridges, opening doors, sorting, taking notes.

## Forbidden

- Don't draw Xiaohei as an overly cute mascot.
- Don't draw a children's cartoon character.
- Don't give Xiaohei complex clothing, meme faces, sparkly eyes.
- Don't let Xiaohei just stand in the corner watching.
- Don't let Xiaohei steal the structural expression.
- Don't draw Xiaohei too commercial, too rounded, too polished.

## Test

If you remove Xiaohei and the image's core metaphor still fully works, Xiaohei is too decorative; rewrite the prompt so Xiaohei becomes the subject of the action.
